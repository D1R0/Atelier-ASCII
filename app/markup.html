More than a filter.

    
A proper instrument for character art, with the parts other converters skip.


    

      

        
Characters chosen by shape, not just darkness

        
Most converters only ask how dark a patch is. Shape mode compares every cell with the outline of each glyph, so a diagonal becomes a slash and a lower edge becomes an underscore. The result reads like something an artist typed.

      

      

    


    

      

        
Portraits written in your own words

        
Pick a name, a quote or a line you wrote, and the image gets drawn with it. Brightness sets how bold each letter looks. Made for gifts, posters and profile headers.

      

      

    


    

      

        
Live video, webcam and recorded clips

        
Point your camera at something and watch it turn into characters in real time. Record up to ten seconds, or loop any still image with effects like decode, rain, scan and wave. Export as a GIF, a video file or a tiny HTML player.

      

      

    


    

      

        
Touch it up by hand

        
Paint characters, erase the background or type a signature straight onto the canvas. Your edits stay in place while you keep adjusting tone and width.

      

      

    

  


  

    
Exports that land in the right shape.

    
Every destination has its own limits. Pick where it's going and Atelier sizes, wraps and encodes it for you.
